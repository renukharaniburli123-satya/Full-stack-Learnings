6-Month Placement Roadmap

Month 1 – Programming Basics

Learn one programming language and practice basic programming problems every day.

Month 2 – Data Structures

Focus on arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees and hashing.

Month 3 – Algorithms

Practice sorting, searching, recursion, greedy algorithms, graphs and dynamic programming.

Month 4 – Core CS Subjects

Revise DBMS, Operating Systems, Computer Networks and Object-Oriented Programming.

Month 5 – Projects & Resume

Build projects, improve your GitHub profile and prepare a clear, professional resume.

Month 6 – Mock Interviews

Practice aptitude tests, coding assessments, technical interviews and HR interviews.

Placement Tips

Practice Daily

Solve coding and aptitude problems consistently instead of studying only before placement tests.

Build Projects

Create real-world projects that demonstrate your programming and problem-solving skills.

Improve Communication

Practice explaining your projects, technical concepts and solutions clearly.

Take Mock Interviews

Participate in mock technical and HR interviews to become comfortable with interview situations.
